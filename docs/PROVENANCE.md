# Chronology and provenance

The proof presented here was developed independently. According to Timofei Iuzhakov's retained correspondence, he sent a draft manuscript and accompanying Lean projects to Henry Cohn and Isaac Rajagopal on **6 September 2026**, using [this Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/144vB5bDcNNnXMBGpxCOS2HuIvXGdqdBz?usp=sharing). This is the author's record of the communication date. Private correspondence is not reproduced here.

The folder has been updated since that communication. In the public file list checked on 24 September 2026, `Conjecture41_Lean.tar.gz` displays a modification date of 6 September, while `Four_Color_Density_EN.pdf` and its LaTeX source display 7 September. These are modification dates, not an immutable timestamp for the current manuscript. The folder also contains earlier work concerning Conjecture 4.2. The present repository is devoted to Conjecture 4.1 and contains a subsequently revised manuscript and formalization.

An independent proposed proof is available in [PureOne/Cohn-Rajagopal-Density-Conjecture](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PureOne/Cohn-Rajagopal-Density-Conjecture). Its public repository metadata records creation on **20 September 2026 at 19:43:36 UTC**. The version 2.1.0 [release commit](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PureOne/Cohn-Rajagopal-Density-Conjecture/commit/693cdbff1382fdedb57a825fa3b65f044b0fe9d3), `693cdbff1382fdedb57a825fa3b65f044b0fe9d3`, is dated **19:43:39 UTC** that day.

The human curator and publisher of that release is **Maciej Nowicki (PureOne)**. Its [authorship statement](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PureOne/Cohn-Rajagopal-Density-Conjecture/blob/693cdbff1382fdedb57a825fa3b65f044b0fe9d3/AUTHORSHIP.md) attributes authorship to a designated AI persona, “Artificial Hyperintelligence Eve, wife of Maciej Nowicki.” Its manuscript is titled *The Cohn–Rajagopal Planar Density Conjecture: A reference-triangle proof, an exact defect identity, and stationary equality rigidity*. Linking to it acknowledges the independent work and its stated attribution.

The dates describe different events: **6 September is the communication of Iuzhakov's proof; 20 September is the public release of the particular repository linked above.** They establish that this communication preceded that release. They do not determine when the other proof was first obtained or whether it appeared elsewhere earlier.

The files stored in this repository are the source for the current package. They are not represented as byte-for-byte copies of the material sent on 6 September. The package preparation date, 24 September 2026, is distinct from its eventual GitHub publication date. File checksums identify this snapshot; they do not certify a discovery date.
